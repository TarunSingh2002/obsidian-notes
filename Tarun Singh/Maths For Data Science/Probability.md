## Simple Probability
- P(A) = 0.3 -> 30 % chance
## Conditional probability
- P(A|B) = Probability of A GIVEN B has already happened
- Formula is  $P(A \mid B) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(B)}$
- Example
	- Question: A card is drawn from a deck. What is the probability it is a King given that it is a face card?
	- Solution:
		- Total card = 52
		- Face card = 12
		- A= 'card is king'
		- B= 'card is a face card'
		- P(B) = 12/52 , $P(A \cap B)$ = 4/52 
		- $P(A \mid B)$ = 1/3
