## What is NLP
- Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a field of artificial intelligence that focuses on the interaction between computers and human languages

## Real World Applications
- Contextual advertisement
- Email spam
- Social media filtration
- Chatbot

## Common Tasks
- Text document classification
- Sentiment analysis
- Info retrieval
- Parts of speech tagging
- Language detection and machine translation
- Conversational agents
- Knowledge graph and QA system
- Text summarization
- Topic modelling
- Text generation
- Spell checking and grammar correction
- Speech to text

---

## Approaches to NLP
- **Heuristic approaches**
	- Regular Expressions
	- WordNet
	- Open Mind Common Sense
- **ML approaches**
	- Naive Bayes
	- Logistic Regression
	- SVM
	- LDA
	- Hidden Markov Model
- **DL approaches**
	- RNN
	- LSTM
	- GRU
	- Transformer
	- Autoencoders

---

## Challenges in NLP
- **Ambiguity**
	- "I saw the boy on the beach with my binoculars" → who has the binoculars?
- **Contextual words**
	- "I ran to the store because we ran out of milk" → the word *ran* has 2 meanings
- **Slang**
	- "pulling your leg"
- Irony, sarcasm
- Spelling errors
- Creativity
- Diversity
